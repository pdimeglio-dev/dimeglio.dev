Google Shopping List needed a frontend that could scale to millions of daily active users across multiple surfaces — Google Search, Google Assistant, and smart home devices — while meeting Google's strict performance and accessibility requirements. The challenge: building a real-time, collaborative shopping experience from scratch.

### Key Achievements
* **Greenfield Development:** Contributed to building the Shopping List frontend from the ground up using Angular, implementing a reactive architecture with RxJS and gRPC for real-time list synchronization across devices.
* **Feature Design Collaboration:** Worked closely with UX designers and the Google tech lead to design and implement key user-facing features, translating complex product requirements into polished, accessible interfaces.
* **Consumer Scale:** Helped engineer the application to reliably serve millions of daily active users, with sub-second response times and graceful degradation under load.
* **Cross-Surface Integration:** Built frontend components that worked seamlessly across Google Search, Google Assistant, and Google Home — adapting the UI for web, mobile, and voice-first interactions.
