Assursoft, based in the Netherlands, needed a web-based solution to manage the central business processes of their medium-to-large insurance brokering firm. Built at VMN+ in La Plata, Argentina, this was one of my first enterprise Java projects — and an early taste of building software for international clients.

### Key Achievements
* **Enterprise Java Platform:** Built a deeply integrated business application using Java, Apache Wicket for the web layer, Hibernate for ORM, and Google Guice for dependency injection.
* **International Client:** Worked with a Netherlands-based client, navigating remote collaboration and business requirements across languages and time zones — skills that would become central to my career.
