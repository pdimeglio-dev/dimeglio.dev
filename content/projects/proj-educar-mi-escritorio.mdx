EducAR is the Argentine government's education portal, part of the [Conectar Igualdad](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conectar_Igualdad) program — a nationwide initiative that distributed laptops to students in public schools across Argentina. The "Mi Escritorio" (My Desktop) application came pre-installed on every laptop, giving children in primary and secondary schools access to thousands of educational resources.

### Key Achievements
* **Social Impact at Scale:** Contributed to an app that reached hundreds of thousands of students across Argentina — one of the few projects in my career where the code directly impacted children's education.
* **Java/Spring Development:** Built features using J2EE, Spring, Spring MVC, and REST services — the same enterprise Java foundations that would later scale through Disney, Google, and beyond.
* **Entry Point to Globant:** This short but meaningful project was where I joined Globant, beginning a 14+ year partnership that would take me from La Plata to San Francisco, working with some of the world's largest companies.
