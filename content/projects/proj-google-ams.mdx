Training AI models at Google's scale requires thousands of labeled data points, but traditional labeling workflows are slow and require deep ML expertise. Agile Modeling Studio needed a frontend that could make Active Learning — the process of iteratively refining a model by having humans rate its predictions — fast, intuitive, and accessible to non-ML researchers.

### Key Achievements
* **Active Learning Workflows:** Built the frontend for Agile-in-an-Hour (AIH), enabling users to rate and refine model predictions through an iterative feedback loop — increasing model training speed by 40%.
* **AIS Evolution:** Developed the frontend for Agile Intelligence Studio, the next-generation platform adding concept exploration, data mining, and LLM-powered solution engineering to the model training toolkit.
* **Google Internal Scale:** The application runs on Google's internal web platform, built with Angular, NgRx, and Protocol Buffers, serving researchers across multiple Google Research teams.
