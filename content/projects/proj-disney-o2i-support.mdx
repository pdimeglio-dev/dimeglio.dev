The O2I (Order to Inventory) suite runs 24/7, handling the global transfer of thousands of high-security entertainment assets — unreleased films, trailers, and marketing materials — via [Aspera Faspex](https://www.ibm.com/products/aspera) and [Signiant](https://www.signiant.com/). When something breaks, it can delay theatrical releases. Production support for this system required deep understanding of the full stack and the ability to diagnose issues under pressure.

### Key Achievements
* **Live System Triage:** Diagnosed and resolved production issues across the Spring Boot backend, Oracle DB, and Aspera/Signiant transfer infrastructure, maintaining uptime for a system critical to Disney's global content pipeline.
* **Splunk Monitoring & Analysis:** Used Splunk for real-time log monitoring, alerting, and forensic analysis — building dashboards and queries to quickly identify issues in the asset transfer pipeline.
* **Root Cause Analysis:** Conducted thorough post-incident analysis, identifying systemic patterns and implementing preventive fixes that reduced recurring issues.
* **System Expertise:** Built deep knowledge of the O2I architecture — from database schemas to transfer protocol internals — which eventually led to the transition into a full stack development role on the same team.
