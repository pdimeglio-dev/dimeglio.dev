PCCW Global was undergoing a strategic transformation — adopting [BillingPlatform](https://billingplatform.com/) as its new integration backbone to reorganize its product catalog and pricing across 150+ countries. The initiative, led by Navint (acquired by Globant), needed a clear microservices architecture to guide the engineering teams through the transition.

### Key Achievements
* **Architecture Design:** Designed the target microservices architecture from the ground up — defining service boundaries using domain-driven design, mapping data flows, and specifying API contracts between services and BillingPlatform.
* **Communication Patterns:** Established standards for inter-service communication, defining when to use synchronous REST vs. asynchronous messaging, along with consistent error handling and retry strategies.
* **BillingPlatform Integration:** Designed the integration layer between existing PCCW services and BillingPlatform, ensuring the product catalog and pricing reorganization could proceed without disrupting active customers.
* **Technical Blueprint:** Delivered comprehensive architectural decision records and design documentation that enabled the broader engineering organization to execute the migration independently.
