Traditional retail search relies on keyword matching — users type "blue dress" and get a list of products sorted by relevance scores. Globant RAISE needed a fundamentally richer experience: multimodal search where users could upload an image of an outfit they liked and receive intelligent recommendations that understood style, color palette, and seasonal trends.

### Key Achievements
* **Multimodal Search:** Built the frontend for a search experience powered by Google Gemini, enabling users to discover products using text, images, or a combination of both — moving beyond keyword matching to true visual and semantic understanding.
* **AI-Powered Recommendations:** Integrated Vertex AI vector search and embeddings to analyze user preferences, browsing history, and current trends, delivering personalized outfit suggestions in real-time.
* **Conversational Agent:** Expanded the platform with a chatbot agent built on DialogFlow CX, adding a conversational layer to the retail discovery experience.
* **NRF 2025 Showcase:** The application was featured at the NRF 2025 conference, the retail industry's largest annual event, demonstrating Globant's AI-driven retail capabilities to enterprise buyers.
