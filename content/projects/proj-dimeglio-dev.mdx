dimeglio.dev is where my work lives, and it's built to be a working AI product rather than a static page. Everything on it, from the experience timeline to the blog, is also the knowledge base for Guillermo, an AI agent you can ask about my work.

### Guillermo, the RAG Agent

Guillermo answers questions about my experience, projects and writing, and only from what's published here.

* **Retrieval first, every time.** Before answering anything about my career, Guillermo searches the portfolio. If the answer isn't there, he says so and offers a way to ask me directly, instead of guessing.
* **Answers as interface, not text dumps.** Projects, skills, blog posts and contact details come back as interactive cards streamed to the page as the answer is written, so a question like "what has he built with React?" returns a filterable list, not a paragraph.
* **From question to conversation.** When a visitor wants to talk, Guillermo shows a contact card or opens a calendar picker with my real availability, and I get an email with the context of the conversation.
* **Guardrails.** Rate limiting per visitor, strict rules on what he can claim, every deep link checked against the content that actually exists, and no salary numbers or opinions about past employers.

### The Knowledge Base

Every experience entry, project, blog post and a set of background notes is turned into embeddings and indexed in Pinecone. Short entries like jobs and projects are indexed whole; long ones like blog posts are split by section, and every piece keeps a header naming the document it came from, so a question about "work at Google" finds the right job and the right projects. Re-indexing is one command, run whenever the content changes.

### The Site

* **Next.js App Router with MDX content.** Experience, projects and posts are plain MDX files, so updating the portfolio is editing text, not code.
* **Diagrams and design.** Architecture diagrams render from Mermaid in a shared visual style, on a dark design system built with Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui and Framer Motion.
* **Observability.** Sentry captures errors on the client and the server and forwards them to PostHog, where they sit next to product analytics, so I can see both what broke and what visitors actually use.

### The Blog Pipeline

The blog is written with a set of Claude Code plugins I built: a reporter reads a project's git history, a writer drafts the post in my voice with a cover image, and an independent rater scores it and can send it back. A social manager drafts the LinkedIn, X and Instagram posts, reviewed by a separate rater. Nothing publishes without my approval.

### Key Achievements

* **A RAG agent in production** that answers from the portfolio only, falls back honestly when it doesn't know, and turns interest into a booked call.
* **Interactive, streamed answers:** cards for projects, skills, posts and contact instead of walls of text.
* **Content as the source of truth:** one set of MDX files powers the site, the agent's knowledge and, next, the generated CV.
* **Full observability** across client and server, with errors and analytics in one place.
* **An agentic writing pipeline** with separate writer and reviewer agents and a human approval step.
