PCCW Global is a Hong Kong-based telecommunications company providing enterprise networking, voice, and data services across 150+ countries. As part of a broader go-to-market strategy led by Navint (acquired by Globant), the company was adopting [BillingPlatform](https://billingplatform.com/) as its new integration backbone to reorganize products and pricing.

As the Microservices Architect and SME, I designed the target microservices architecture — defining service boundaries, inter-service communication patterns, and integration points with BillingPlatform. The work was pure architectural design: establishing the technical blueprint that the engineering teams would follow to modernize the platform around the new billing integration.
