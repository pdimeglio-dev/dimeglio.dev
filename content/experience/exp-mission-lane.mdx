Mission Lane is a fintech company building credit cards and financial products for underserved consumers — people rebuilding credit or accessing financial services for the first time. Their platform handles sensitive financial data with zero-downtime requirements, strict compliance, and the reliability that banking regulators demand.

As Full Stack Tech Lead, I led a distributed team of 5+ engineers through a monolith-to-microservices migration on GCP — building the consumer-facing UI in React while decomposing legacy banking systems into Kotlin microservices. I owned the end-to-end delivery across the full stack while establishing observability, CI/CD, and infrastructure-as-code practices that improved system reliability across the platform.
