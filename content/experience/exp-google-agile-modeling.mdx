Google Research's Agile Modeling Studio (AMS) is an internal platform that makes AI model training accessible to non-ML experts. The core product, Agile-in-an-Hour (AIH), lets researchers introduce new labels into Media Understanding Models — tools like Agile Image Classification (ICA) — through Active Learning: an iterative loop where humans rate model predictions to progressively improve accuracy.

As a senior frontend engineer on the team, I built the complex frontend architecture for AMS and its next-generation successor, Agile Intelligence Studio (AIS). AIS expanded the platform with concept exploration, data mining, and LLM-powered solution engineering — giving researchers a unified interface to train, evaluate, and refine models at Google Research scale.
