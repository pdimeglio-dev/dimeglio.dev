Wells Fargo's Store Vision Platform is the internal system used by thousands of in-branch bankers to manage customer interactions, product recommendations, and branch operations. The platform was built on legacy jQuery and needed a modern frontend to support evolving business requirements.

As a full stack engineer on the modernization team, I contributed to migrating the frontend from jQuery to Polymer — where we encountered and helped debug critical IE memory leaks in the Polymer framework — before pivoting to Angular as the long-term target architecture. The work directly improved the daily experience of thousands of store bankers across the country.
