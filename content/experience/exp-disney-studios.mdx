The Walt Disney Studios manages some of the most valuable entertainment assets in the world — from feature films and trailers to behind-the-scenes content and marketing materials. The O2I (Order to Inventory) suite handles the secure global transfer of these high-sensitivity assets between studios, post-production houses, and distribution partners.

I joined the team as a Production Support Engineer, diagnosing and resolving live production issues for the O2I suite — where downtime could delay theatrical releases. Over time, I transitioned into a full stack development role, building new features in Java and Angular while continuing to provide the deep system knowledge that comes from living in the production trenches.
