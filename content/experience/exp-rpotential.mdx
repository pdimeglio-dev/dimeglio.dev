The company helps enterprises deploy agentic AI for complex recruitment workflows, workforce simulation, and human-AI workforce integration at scale.

The core engineering challenge: build AI-first platforms where every interface is generated dynamically by Large Language Models in real-time — no predefined layouts, no static pages, no traditional frontend patterns.

As Tech Lead, I own the end-to-end engineering execution across three interconnected platforms: a Generative UI engine, a shared component library for AI-composed interfaces, and an internal AI developer assistant. Leading a distributed team, I championed an AI-augmented Software Delivery Life Cycle using Claude and Copilot, compressing delivery timelines from months to weeks — shipping complete full-stack platforms within tight 4-week cycles at 300% of the team's original velocity.
