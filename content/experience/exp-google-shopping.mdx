Google Shopping List is a consumer-facing product used by millions of daily active users to create, manage, and share shopping lists — integrated across Google Search, Google Assistant, and Google Home devices. The product sits at the intersection of commerce, search, and the smart home ecosystem.

As a frontend engineer embedded in the Google Shopping team, I contributed to building the brand-new Shopping List frontend application from the ground up — collaborating closely with UX designers and the Google tech lead to design key features and engineer an architecture that reliably served millions of users while meeting Google's exacting standards for performance, accessibility, and internationalization.
