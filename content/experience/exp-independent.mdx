Alongside my day job, I build and run my own products, from the idea and the design to the infrastructure, the release and the people using them.

The Paddle Games is a leaderboard and challenge platform for Bay Area paddlers. It syncs activities from Strava, scores them with a data-driven rules engine, and runs location-based route challenges with personal records. It's live on the web and as a native iPhone app on the App Store, backed by seven event-driven services on Google Cloud, with close to 100 paddlers and a community on Instagram.

Batcave is my personal multi-agent system. A router sends each message to one of three agents, each running its own Claude Agent SDK loop with its own tools and cost limits: Alfred for conversation and memory, Gordon for nutrition, and Ra's al Ghul for CrossFit training. I reach them through Telegram voice, an iPhone and Apple Watch app, and two web apps. It runs on Cloud Run, deploys on every merge behind eval gates, and has its own Claude Code agents that triage bugs and open fix pull requests for me to review.
