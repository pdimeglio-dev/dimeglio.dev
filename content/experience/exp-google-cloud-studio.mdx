Globant RAISE (Retail AI Services) is an application built to showcase Globant's innovative Retail Studio offerings at the NRF 2025 conference — the retail industry's largest annual event. The platform demonstrates how Google's Generative AI capabilities can transform the retail experience through multimodal product discovery and intelligent recommendations.

As the senior frontend engineer, I designed and implemented the UI that brings these AI capabilities to life — enabling users to search retail catalogs using text, images, or a combination of both, powered by Google Gemini and Vertex AI. The application delivers personalized outfit suggestions based on current trends, user profiles, and browsing history, improving multimodal search efficiency by 50%, with an expanding conversational agent built on DialogFlow CX.
