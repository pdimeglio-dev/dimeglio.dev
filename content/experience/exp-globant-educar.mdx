EducAR is the Argentine government's education portal, part of the Conectar Igualdad program that distributed laptops to schoolchildren across the country. The "Mi Escritorio" application came pre-installed on these laptops, providing kids in primary and secondary schools access to thousands of educational resources.

This was my entry point into Globant — I joined as a Senior Java Developer collaborating on the Mi Escritorio app using J2EE, Spring, Spring MVC, and REST services. Shortly after, I transitioned to the Disney Parks team, beginning a 14+ year journey with Globant that would take me from La Plata to the San Francisco Bay Area, working with Disney, Google, and beyond.
