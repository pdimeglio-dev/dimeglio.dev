Walt Disney Parks and Resorts Online builds the digital experiences that millions of guests use to plan, book, and enjoy their Disney vacations — from resort reservations and dining to park tickets and FastPass. The backend services power everything from the web and mobile apps to in-park kiosks.

As a backend engineer, I developed highly scalable RESTful Web Services using Java, Spring, and JPA that served millions of annual Disney park guests. This was my entry point into enterprise-scale software engineering — building systems where reliability and performance directly impacted the guest experience at Disney's parks worldwide.
