## Origin Story

Pablo was born in La Plata, Argentina, but grew up in San Clemente del Tuyú — a small coastal town in Buenos Aires Province. He studied Information Systems Engineering and Systems Analysis at Universidad Tecnológica Nacional (UTN FRLP), graduating with both degrees between 2004 and 2009.

He started his professional career in La Plata working at VMN+ (a small consultancy), then Accenture, then Globant. In 2011 he joined Globant as a Senior Java Developer on the EducAR project for the Argentine government, and shortly after transitioned to the Disney Parks team.

## The US Journey

In 2014, Globant offered Pablo an onsite position in the United States with an L1 visa. He moved to Los Angeles and joined the Walt Disney Studios team, working on production support and full stack development for Disney's global asset transfer system.

From there the career progressed through some of the most recognized companies in the world:
